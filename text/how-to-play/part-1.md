# How to Play/Part 1

### Equipment

To play go you need a square grid and black and white stones. A standard grid is 19x19 and printed on a board. Smaller board sizes that are also regular are 13x13, 9x9 and to a lesser extent 7x7 and 5x5. 

### Basics

Stones are placed on the intersections of the board, not the spaces. Once a stone is played it does not move. Black and white alternate taking turns placing a single stone on the board. More on who plays first later.

### Purpose
The object of the game is to surround territory. More territory than your opponent. A point of territory is an unoccupied intersection of the board surrounded by a single color of stones. In this 5x5 game, black has 5 more points of territory than white.

Stones can also be captured on the sides and corners of the board. Previously, to capture the stone in the middle of the board it took black four stones. To capture these black stones on the side white only needs to use three stones, on the corner two stones. It is easier to capture a stone against walls of the board than in the center.
