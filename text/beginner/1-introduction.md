# Introduction

Go is a strategy board game. Originating in China (there it is called weiqi), it is also vastly popular in Japan (called igo) and Korea (called baduk). So much so that there are dedicated schools for training young professionals in the game. While go is certainly most prevalent in these countries, it has spread across the world as a favorite hobby and other cultures have developed their own go associations and professional scenes.  

In this guide I mostly use English and Japanese terms, and Japanese rules.

Go is both incredibly simple and sophisticated. There are two video resources I encourage you to watch to help you appreciate the elegance of go. First, the documentary AlphaGo, presently available to watch for free on YouTube. [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXuK6gekU1Y). Second, Nick Sibicky's episode titled, "What I REALLY Like About Go". [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRveBqnPJKg)
