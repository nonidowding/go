# How to Play/Part 1

### Equipment

To play go you need a square grid and black and white stones. A standard grid is 19x19 and printed on a board. Smaller board sizes that are also regular are 13x13, 9x9 and to a lesser extent 7x7 and 5x5. 

### Purpose
The object of the game is to surround territory. More territory than your opponent. A point of territory is an unoccupied intersection of the board surrounded by a single color of stones.
