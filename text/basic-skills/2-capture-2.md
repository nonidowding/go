# Basic/Capture Level 2
## New Rules
To take capture go to the next level and make it more difficult, change the objective. All of the previous rules are still in effect except now you must capture at least 3 stones at once to win. This means first capture does not necessarily win. If you only capture 1 or 2 stones play continues. 

You may also now pass your turn. If you don't see any beneficial moves left, you are not obligated to play. If both players pass in succession the game ends in a draw. 

I suggest playing with this rule set on a 9x9 board.
