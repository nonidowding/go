# How to Play/Part 1

### Equipment

To play go you need a square grid and two colors of stones. Traditionally, the grid is a 19x19 board and the stones are black and white. Smaller sizes that are also regular are 13x13, 9x9 and to a lesser extent 7x7 and 5x5. If playing with a physical set, the stones should be kept in a container with a lid that can also contain a handful of stones.
