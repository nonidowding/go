# Basic Skills/Capture
## Overview
Go is a two person game. It is played on a square grid called a board. The standard board size is 19x19, with 13x13 and 9x9 as smaller options. In this section of the guide I will be using 7x7 and 5x5, considered teaching sizes.

The pieces are black and white stones. A full go set has 181 black stones and 180 white stones. But it's OK if you have a few less.

Stones are played on the intersections of the board, not the spaces. Once a stone is placed it does not move position and cannot be taken back. 

First, I'm going to teach you how to play a very beginner friendly variant of go called "capture go". The rules are simple: 

1. At the Start of the game the board is empty.
2. Black plays first. They may play anywhere to start.
3. White and black then alternate turns placing a single stone on the board. For the most part they may play anywhere but there is a case of an [illegal move](#illegal-move).
4. Either player may pass their turn at any time. 
5. Whoever makes the first capture wins.

Stones can also be captured on the sides and corners of the board. Note that in the middle of the board it took four stones to capture 1 stone. On the side of the board it takes 3 stones to capture 1 stone, and on the corner it only takes two stones to capture 1 stone. It is easier to capture against walls of the board than in the center.
