# Basic/Capture Level 2
## New Rules
Let's up the difficulty level by modifying the rules to capture go. All of the previous rules are still in effect except now you must capture at least 3 stones at once to win. This means first capture does not necessarily win, if you only capture 1 or 2 stones play continues. 

This also creates opportunity for situations that require defining a [2nd illegal move]().

I suggest playing with this rule set on a 9x9 board.
