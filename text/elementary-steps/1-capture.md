# How to Play/Part 1

Go is played on a square grid usually printed on a board. The pieces are black and white stones. Stones are played on the intersections of the board, not the spaces. Once a stone is placed it does not move. 

Using what is presented in this part of the guide, it is possible to play an abbreviated game called "capture go". The rules are simple: 

1. Black plays first. They may play anywhere to start.
2. White and black then alternate turns placing a single stone on the board. For the most part they may play anywhere, there is a case of an [illegal move](#illegal-move).
3. Whoever makes the first capture wins.

Stones can also be captured on the sides and corners of the board. To capture the white stone in the middle of the board it took black four stones. To capture these black stones on the side white only needs to use three stones, on the corner two stones. It is easier to capture a stone against walls of the board than in the center.
